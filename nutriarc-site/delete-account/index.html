How to permanently delete your NutriArc account and data

You can delete your NutriArc account at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open NutriArc and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Your account and all associated data are removed immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, email lunabhattarai100@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, with the subject “NutriArc account deletion request”.

We will verify the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

All of the following are permanently erased:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile — name, age, sex, height, weight, goals and activity level
  • All food logs, meals, calories and macros
  • All workout plans and training history
  • All body metrics and progress history
  • Your profile photo

What is retained

Nothing personal is retained after deletion. Anonymous, aggregated records that cannot identify you may remain in backups for up to 30 days before being overwritten, and we may keep records where retention is required by law.
