Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

NutriArc ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account information — email address and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth)
  • Profile data — name, age, sex, height, weight, fitness goal, and activity level you provide during onboarding
  • Food logs — meals, calories, and macros you log in the app
  • Health metrics — weight entries and water intake you record
  • Food images — photos you take to scan food (processed by our AI; not stored permanently)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To calculate your personalised calorie and macro targets
  • To display your nutrition history, progress charts, and streaks
  • To identify food items via AI (images are sent to our server, analysed, then discarded)
  • To maintain your account and authenticate you securely

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider (data stored in the US)
  • Anthropic — food image and text analysis (Claude AI; images are not retained by Anthropic beyond the request)

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are never stored in plaintext. Database access is protected by row-level security policies.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

NutriArc is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us at privacy@nutriarc.app
